# Acceptance RC16.31bm

RC16.31bm is accepted when a live Norway-only run proves all of the following:

- all configured Norwegian symbols are still covered by the existing discovery/pipeline contract;
- `early_signal_watchlist` is built from analysed candidates and is not merely a copy of investment Top 10;
- accelerating 5/10/20d momentum, SMA20/50/200 structure, golden cross, 20/60d breakout, RSI and volume/OBV signals are recorded when data supports them;
- market and sector relative-strength percentiles are shown;
- the UI explains why the signal may support continuation, the next confirmation checks, and the failure/caution checks;
- RSI overbought conditions are shown as a caution rather than as automatic bullish evidence;
- trend charts remain readable with 20d/60d, breakout levels, RSI 30/50/70 and volume;
- no early signal changes investment score, buy threshold, risk ceiling, portfolio gate or trade authorisation;
- no OOM regression is introduced in manual reports or scheduler runs.
